How much caffeine is in one serving?

A full serving contains 200 mg. Begin with half a scoop to assess tolerance, and do not combine it with other caffeine or stimulants.

Can I take more than one scoop?

No. The stated maximum is one full 10 g serving in 24 hours. Individual tolerance varies.

Why can beta-alanine cause tingling?

Beta-alanine can cause temporary skin tingling. Stop use and seek professional advice if you experience a concerning reaction.
